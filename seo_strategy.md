# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages (`/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/solutions`, `/contact`)
- Public blog pages (`/blog`, `/blog/:slug`)
- Public resource/tool pages (`/files`, `/resources`, `/tools/epm-comparison`)
- Public FinanceCompass marketing/help routes (`/finance-compass`, `/finance-compass/methodology`, `/finance-compass/chat-popup`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated admin routes (`/admin/**`)
- Session-specific or authenticated FinanceCompass app surfaces (`/finance-compass/dashboard/**`, `/finance-compass/assess/**`, `/finance-compass/results/**`, `/finance-compass/roi/**`)
- API endpoints except where they directly control crawlability assets such as `robots.txt` or `sitemap.xml`

## Target audience
- Finance leaders evaluating EPM, FP&A, finance transformation, and AI advisory services.

## Primary keywords
- EPM advisory
- Finance transformation
- Finance systems integration
- EPM platform comparison
- Finance maturity assessment

## Dismissed categories
- None yet.

## Notes
- Current public experience is a Vite/React SPA served by Express.
- Source indicates traditional search, social sharing, and AI crawler visibility all matter for this project.
